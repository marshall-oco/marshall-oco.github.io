# Marshall's observatory

One person's observatory: every constellation and star he tends, seen as one sky - published at
<https://marshall-oco.github.io/>.

It is made with OCO, and is not OCO: anyone may have an observatory. Its mark (`oco.toml`) says what it
gathers - OCO itself among the constellations it tracks - and it observes only that. Every few hours,
and whenever asked, it observes what has changed in those places and lays the fulldome out on the page:
every lens, in focus, over what was observed.

    ocoeco fulldome            # here: this observatory and everything it gathers
